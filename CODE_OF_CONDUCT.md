# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SysPerf to be a project where contributing is straightforward and
disagreement is about the work. Everyone participating — issues, pull requests,
discussions — is expected to help keep it that way, regardless of experience
level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and considerate about the person.
- Critique code, measurements and reasoning, not people.
- Accept that "no" is sometimes the right answer to a well-made proposal, and say
  why when you give it.
- Assume the other person is trying to make the tool better.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private security advisory on the
repository or contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers will respond with a warning, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban, proportionate to the incident, and may remove content that
violates this policy.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face consequences decided by the other maintainers.
